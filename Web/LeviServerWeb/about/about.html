About Me

Electronics, Programming, Amateur Radio and Low Level Systems

Hi, I’m Ishir. I develop software and build electronics, ranging from analog circuits to digital systems. I'm interested in low-level architecture, which often leads me to design or follow digital circuits and use languages like Assembly or C++. Additionally, I enjoy amateur radio, which also influences what projects I work on.

This site serves as a technical journal of my engineering journey. It documents most of my projects, including my problems, the examples I create, and the schematics I design. I try to make my projects accessible by providing documentation, diagrams, and explanations for everything I build.
